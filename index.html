R&D PROJECT

Kitchen Garden

Automated Vertical Hydroponic Farming System

A soil-free vertical farm engineered for the modern home. The system combines app-controlled lighting, automated nutrient cycles and water management, with a focus on creating a compact and automated consumer-hardware experience.

Project Kitchen Garden
Role R&D Design Engineer
Team 5-Member Cross-Functional Team
Product Type Automated Hydroponic System
Overview

Bringing Automated Farming Into the Home

Kitchen Garden was developed as a compact, soil-free vertical farming system intended for residential environments.

The product concept focused on integrating automated lighting, nutrient-management cycles and water-management functions into a system designed around simplicity and usability.

The development approach aimed to combine agricultural functionality with the design philosophy of premium consumer hardware.

My Role

R&D Coordination & Technical Direction

I was responsible for coordinating a 5-member cross-functional team involved in the development of the Kitchen Garden.

My responsibilities included team coordination, development management, establishing technical direction and contributing to key product and design decisions.

I also worked across different functions to align technical activities and maintain a structured development workflow throughout the project lifecycle.
