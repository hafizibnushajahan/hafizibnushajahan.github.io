R&D PROJECT

Kitchen Garden

Automated Vertical Hydroponic Farming System

A soil-free vertical farm engineered for the modern home. The system combines app-controlled lighting, automated nutrient cycles and water management, with a focus on delivering the experience of premium consumer hardware.

Project Type Consumer Hardware / Hydroponics
Role R&D Design Engineer
Team 5-Member Cross-Functional Team
Development Stage Prototype → Validation → Handover

Technical Leadership & Product Development

I was responsible for coordinating a 5-member cross-functional team working on the development of the Kitchen Garden product.
